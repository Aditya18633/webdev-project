About me

I’m a Computer Science & AI student currently studying in Bangalore, with a growing interest in technology, programming, and artificial intelligence. My journey into this field started with curiosity about how websites, applications, and the technology we use every day actually work. As I continue learning, I’m building my fundamentals step by step and trying to understand not only how things work, but also why they work.

I come from karmala,Maharashtra where I completed my schooling at Mahatma Gandhi Vidyalay karmala. Moving to Bangalore for my higher studies has given me the opportunity to explore a completely new environment, meet different people, and experience a different side of technology and education. It has also encouraged me to become more independent and take more responsibility for my own learning.

Currently, I’m focusing on strengthening my programming and computer science fundamentals while exploring AI and different areas of technology. I believe that the best way to learn is by actually building things, which is why I’ve started creating small projects along the way. One of my projects is an Amazon-inspired e-commerce website, where I used HTML and CSS to practice creating layouts, structuring webpages, and bringing a design idea to life.

I’m still at the beginning of my journey, so I don’t have everything figured out yet — and that’s something I’m comfortable with. I enjoy experimenting, making mistakes, figuring out what went wrong, and improving from there. My goal is to keep learning, keep building, and gradually turn my ideas into projects that are useful and meaningful.
